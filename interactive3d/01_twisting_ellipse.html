Controls & mathematical notes

Mouse: drag to orbit; wheel to zoom; right-drag or Shift-drag to pan. Touch: one finger rotates; pinch zooms; two fingers pan. Space pauses when focus is outside a control. Keys 1–4 select scenes. The timeline pauses playback when dragged. Reset view changes only the camera.

These are geometric constructions and analytic formulas, not particle simulations. The channel walls are stationary. Axial length is schematic. Orange arrows are the transverse cell-gradient field, with fixed display scaling. The conformal map and the bounds are mathematical planes embedded in the 3D viewer; rotating them adds no physical variable. FEM points are numerical estimates, not certified error bars.
